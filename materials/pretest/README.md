# Pretest instrument

The manipulation check that validated the four stimulus texts before production.

`instrument.md` is the questionnaire as raters met it, generated from the SoSci
exports in `data/raw/Pretest/`: six questions across 53 variables, covering the
three target subscales, the two control scales and the demographics.

`screenshots/` holds ten screenshots of the live pages, which show the visual
presentation that the generated instrument cannot.

Responses are in `data/processed/pretest_*.csv`, the raw exports in
`data/raw/Pretest/`.
